Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 16, 2026

Introduction

Privacy is an important concern, and the security of our users' data is important to us...

A Public Layer on the Internet

Stllr.io is a public layer designed on top of the internet. As such, users operate our services under the agreement that their activity is in the public domain...

Data We Collect

The data stllr.io collects is limited to user profile data, star actions, forum posts, and room presence. This data is already assumed by the user to be public data and therefore available to all third parties... Users may delete their own star actions and forum posts, thereby removing them from their public-facing profile and all pages. However, these objects may still be persisted in our databases to enable our review of users' compliance with our user agreement.Email verification is mandatory on sign up, helping us validate human users and unique account identities.

How We Collect Your Data

Cookies

Stllr.io uses cookies to persist user sessions, meaning users do not need to log in every time they open our website or our browser extension. We do not use cookies to track user actions or browsing across websites. The data that stllr.io collects with regard to user activity is limited to those actions that are already agreed to be public knowledge: star actions, forum posts, and room presence... CSRF Protection. Cookies are session identifiers => no personal data is stored in the cookies themselves.

What We Do With Your Data

data we collect is only used insofar that it enables our Service (providing star buttons, forums, and rooms on webpages across the internet.)

Third Party Services

Services we use may have access to user account data, such as hosting provider DigitalOcean. CCPA (California) right to know what's sold/shared and opt out

Controlling Your Data

You can edit your profile, change your username, email, and background photo. You can remove user actions (i.e. delete stars, posts, and messages) but these objects may be stored in our database for at least 30 days to enable review of users' compliance with our user agreement.

Retrieving Your Data

Users that wish to retrieve all data associated with their account may email us and their data will be sent to them within thirty days at the email they have on file.

Children

Children disclaimer: We do not knowingly obtain personal data from minors... State minimum age (13+) so COPPA does not apply.

Updates to this Policy

This policy may be updated at any time. We encourage users to frequently return to this page for updates. We may alert users to changes with displays on our websites or via email.
